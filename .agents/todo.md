## What a team that holds a role for weeks still lacks

From [standing teams](notes/proposed/2026-11-12-standing-teams.md), as it stands now.

- **Remembering together.** Each agent has its own memory, so a new worker learns the
  project again. Proposed: [shared team memory](notes/proposed/2026-11-12-shared-team-memory.md).
- **Not paying while nothing happens.** A duty checks in on a timer (at most every 5
  minutes), and each check-in is a request, also when nothing changed.
- **Asking before acting on the outside world.** The sandbox keeps writes in the work
  folder and leaves the network open: on Auto or work-folder permissions a command can
  post, send or pay through an API without asking.
- **Reaching the person with no window open.** Notifications come from the window; with
  it closed, a question or an approval waits unseen.
- **Coming back after a reboot.** Every fleet stops, and none starts until the person
  resumes it.
